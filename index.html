What we do

Find it · build it · teach it
Step 1

Find the busywork

We sit with your team and map where the hours go: scheduling, follow-ups, data entry, the same email written forty times. You get a short list of what is worth automating and what is not.

Step 2

Build the fix

We build the agent or automation around the tools you already use, then test it the way we test our own code: repeated runs, checks that are built to fail, and a log of every action it takes.

Step 3

Teach your team

We hand it over in plain English and coach your people to use AI well in their own work, so you are not calling us for every small change.
